Tomato soup is the classic partner for grilled cheese for a good reason. It is warm and comforting, and its acidity cuts through the richness of the cheese and butter. The same idea works for far more than soup. Once you know why the pairing works, you can build a plate that never gets boring.

## The rule: balance the richness

A grilled cheese is rich, salty and soft. The best sides push back with at least one of these:

- **Acid:** tomatoes, vinegar, pickles, citrus.
- **Crunch:** crisp vegetables, chips, fries.
- **Freshness:** raw greens, herbs, cold salads.
- **A little sweetness:** fruit, roasted vegetables, a sweet-tart dip.

Pick one or two, and the sandwich tastes better for it.

## Soups beyond tomato

- **Roasted red pepper.** Sweet and slightly tangy, so it behaves much like tomato soup.
- **Butternut squash.** Creamy and sweet. It goes especially well with a sharp cheddar sandwich.
- **Chicken noodle.** Pure comfort, and it makes the plate a full meal.
- **Minestrone or vegetable soup.** Light, fresh and full of vegetables.
- **Lentil soup.** Earthy and filling.
- **Gazpacho.** A cold, tangy option for a hot day.

## Salads and slaws

A crisp, sharply dressed salad is the easiest way to balance a grilled cheese.

- A simple green salad with a lemon or vinegar dressing.
- Arugula with lemon and a little olive oil.
- A crunchy cabbage slaw.
- Cucumber salad.

## Pickles and other sharp things

Anything pickled does the same job as the acid in tomato soup.

- Dill pickles and cornichons.
- Pickled red onions.
- Giardiniera or banana peppers.
- Hot sauce or whole-grain mustard for dipping.

## Fruit

Apple and cheddar are an old favorite, so apple slices on the side make sense. Pear slices and grapes work for the same reason: they are sweet, juicy and a little tart. Some people add a thin spread of jam or chutney inside the sandwich for the same effect.

## Something crunchy

- Kettle-style potato chips.
- Oven fries or roasted potatoes.
- Roasted vegetables such as broccoli or Brussels sprouts.
- Crispy roasted chickpeas.

## Dips and extras

Ketchup is perfectly fine. If you want something different, try mustard, pesto, chutney or a drizzle of hot honey.

## Drinks

Crisp, cold drinks do well with rich food: iced tea with lemon, lemonade, or sparkling water with citrus. If you are pouring something alcoholic, a crisp lager, a dry cider or a dry white wine is a classic match.

## Three easy plates

- **Quick lunch:** grilled cheese, dill pickles and apple slices.
- **Cozy dinner:** grilled cheese, roasted red pepper soup and a simple green salad.
- **Summer meal:** grilled cheese, cucumber salad and iced tea, or a small bowl of gazpacho.
